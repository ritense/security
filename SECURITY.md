# Security Policy

## Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure

At Ritense, the security of our products, services and infrastructure is important to us. Despite our efforts to maintain a high level of security, vulnerabilities may still occur.

If you discover a security vulnerability in a Ritense system, service or product, we encourage you to report it to us. We appreciate the efforts of security researchers who help us improve our security and ask that you follow the guidelines below.

## Scope

This policy applies to:

- Publicly accessible systems and services owned or managed by Ritense;
- Ritense products and software;
- Ritense-managed cloud environments, including customer environments hosted or managed by Ritense;
- Ritense open-source software and public source-code repositories.

Systems, services or infrastructure operated by third parties and for which Ritense has no ability or authority to remediate vulnerabilities are outside the scope of this policy.

If you are unsure whether a system is in scope, please contact us before conducting further research.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report suspected vulnerabilities to **[security@ritense.com](mailto:security@ritense.com)**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Please do **not** report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues, discussions or pull requests.

Please include as much of the following information as possible:

- A description of the vulnerability;
- The affected product, system, repository or URL;
- Steps required to reproduce the vulnerability;
- The potential security impact;
- Relevant proof-of-concept code, screenshots or other supporting information;
- Your contact details so that we can contact you about the report.

Please do not include more confidential or personal information than is necessary to demonstrate the vulnerability.

## Research guidelines

When investigating a potential vulnerability, we ask you to act responsibly and in good faith.

In particular:

- Only perform actions necessary to identify and demonstrate the vulnerability;
- Do not exploit a vulnerability beyond what is necessary to demonstrate its existence;
- Do not perform denial-of-service (DoS or DDoS) attacks or otherwise intentionally disrupt our services;
- Do not use social engineering, phishing or attacks against Ritense employees, customers or other individuals;
- Do not install malware, ransomware, backdoors or other persistent access mechanisms;
- Do not modify, delete or intentionally corrupt data;
- Do not access, download, copy or retain personal, confidential or otherwise sensitive information beyond what is strictly necessary to demonstrate the vulnerability;
- Do not attempt to compromise physical security;
- Do not use automated testing in a manner that could degrade the availability or performance of our systems;
- Delete any data obtained during your research as soon as it is no longer required for reporting the vulnerability;
- Do not share information about the vulnerability with third parties before disclosure has been coordinated with Ritense.

If you unexpectedly gain access to sensitive information or functionality beyond what is necessary to demonstrate the vulnerability, stop your investigation and report the issue to us.

## What you can expect from us

When you report a vulnerability in accordance with this policy, Ritense will strive to:

- Acknowledge receipt of your report within **3 business days**;
- Perform an initial assessment and provide a substantive response within **10 business days**;
- Keep you informed about relevant progress where appropriate;
- Prioritize remediation based on the severity, impact and complexity of the vulnerability;
- Work with you to coordinate any disclosure of the vulnerability.

If you believe we have inadvertently fallen short of these commitments, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for clarification or to kindly follow up.

Remediation times may differ depending on the severity and complexity of the vulnerability. We therefore do not guarantee a fixed remediation period.

Where appropriate, Ritense may publish a security advisory and/or request or publish a CVE for vulnerabilities affecting Ritense products.

## Coordinated disclosure

We support responsible publication of security research.

Please do not publicly disclose a vulnerability before it has been discussed and coordinated with Ritense. Ritense and the researcher will, where applicable, agree on an appropriate disclosure approach and timing, taking into account the time required to remediate the vulnerability and protect affected users.

After appropriate remediation or mitigation, publication of the vulnerability may be coordinated with Ritense.

## Safe harbour

Ritense will not initiate legal action against researchers who:

- Conduct their research in good faith;
- Comply with this Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure Policy;
- Avoid unnecessary harm to Ritense, our customers and other parties; and
- Report discovered vulnerabilities to us without unnecessary delay.

If you accidentally exceed the boundaries described in this policy, stop the relevant activity and notify us immediately. Ritense will take the circumstances, intent and actions taken to prevent or limit harm into account when assessing the situation.

This safe-harbour statement does not apply to deliberate malicious activity, extortion, threats, intentional disruption, deliberate privacy violations or other actions clearly outside the purpose and boundaries of good-faith security research.

## Rewards

Ritense may, at its sole discretion, provide an appropriate reward for a valuable vulnerability report.

The type and value of any reward depend on factors such as the severity, impact and quality of the report. Reporting a vulnerability does not create an entitlement to a financial or other reward.

## Questions

If you are unsure whether your intended research complies with this policy, please contact us at [security@ritense.com](mailto:security@ritense.com) before proceeding.

Thank you for helping us keep Ritense, our products and our customers secure.
